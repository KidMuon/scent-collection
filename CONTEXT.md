# Scent Collection

A personal catalogue of colognes: what each one smells like, what it evokes, and when it suits being worn. It exists to help decide what to wear and to spot gaps in the collection.

## Language

### Colognes

**Cologne**:
Any fragrance tracked in the app, regardless of its Concentration.
_Avoid_: Fragrance, perfume, scent, juice

**Concentration**:
How a Cologne is formulated: Eau de Cologne, Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Parfum, Extrait. "Eau de Cologne" is a Concentration, never a synonym for Cologne.
_Avoid_: Strength

**House**:
The brand that makes a Cologne.
_Avoid_: Brand, designer, maker

**Ownership status**:
Whether a Cologne is Owned, Wishlisted, or Gone (sold, given away, or used up). Every Cologne has exactly one.
_Avoid_: State, sample, decant

**Collection**:
The Colognes whose Ownership status is Owned. Searching and gap-finding look at the Collection unless asked to include Wishlisted or Gone Colognes.
_Avoid_: Inventory, library, wardrobe

### Character

**Accord**:
An impression a Cologne evokes, such as Tobacco, Fresh Spicy or Southern Rain. Accords come from a starting list or are created by the user; once in the list, both kinds are the same thing. Each Accord has a color.
_Avoid_: Vibe, family, mood

**Accord strength**:
How prominent an Accord is within one Cologne, from 1 to 20. It is relative to that Cologne's other Accords, so it says nothing about how strong the Accord is compared with other Colognes.
_Avoid_: Intensity, weight, percentage

**Note**:
A material the user smells in a Cologne, such as bergamot or tobacco leaf. Notes are what a Cologne smells of; Accords are what it makes the user feel.
_Avoid_: Ingredient

**Longevity**:
How long a Cologne lasts once applied, from 1 to 5.

**Projection**:
How far a Cologne radiates from the wearer, from 1 to 5.
_Avoid_: Sillage, performance

### When to wear

**Season**:
One of Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter.
_Avoid_: Autumn

**Time of day**:
Either Day or Night.

### Deciding

**Label**:
A tag the user creates, gives a color, and attaches to Colognes to help decide what to wear, such as "office-safe" or "date night". Only the user applies Labels; anything the app computes is not a Label. Some overlap with Accords is expected and fine.
_Avoid_: Tag, category
