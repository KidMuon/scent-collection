# Accord strengths are relative within a Cologne

Accord strength (1–20) records how prominent an Accord is compared with the other Accords in the same Cologne, the way Fragrantica's bar widths do. It is not an absolute scale shared across the Collection. The user copies starting values from Fragrantica and similar sites, and doesn't trust their own nose to judge absolute strength consistently across many Colognes.

## Consequences

- Tobacco at 15 in two Colognes doesn't mean they are equally tobacco-forward. Anything that compares Colognes, search ranking included, can only treat strength as "how prominent within this Cologne".
- Values entered this way can't be converted to an absolute scale later.
- By convention the strongest Accord is entered as 20 and bars are drawn at strength ÷ 20. The app doesn't enforce the convention; whether to enforce or rescale is open until the user has tried it.
